Air Fryer Chicken Thighs

By Recipe Team · Published 2026-07-17 · 27g protein per serving

Chicken thighs are close to foolproof in the air fryer — the fat under the skin renders and crisps in the moving hot air without any need to babysit an oven.

Twenty-two minutes and barely any cleanup gets you crispier skin than most oven methods manage in twice the time.

Don't crowd the basket — pieces need space between them for air to circulate, which is the entire mechanism behind an air fryer's crisping power.

Air Fryer Chicken Thighs

Prep: 5 min Cook: 22 min Total: 27 min Yield: 4 servings 320 cal · 27g protein

Ingredients

  • 6 bone-in, skin-on chicken thighs
  • 1 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 tsp garlic powder
  • 1 tsp smoked paprika
  • 1/2 tsp onion powder
  • Salt and pepper, to taste

Instructions

  1. 1. Season the chicken

    Pat the chicken thighs dry with paper towels. Rub with olive oil, then coat evenly with garlic powder, smoked paprika, onion powder, salt and pepper.

  2. 2. Preheat and arrange

    Preheat the air fryer to 380°F (193°C). Arrange the thighs skin-side down in a single layer in the basket, without overlapping.

  3. 3. Air fry

    Cook for 12 minutes, then flip skin-side up and cook for another 10-12 minutes, until the skin is crisp and a thermometer reads 175°F (79°C).

  4. 4. Rest and serve

    Let rest for 3-5 minutes before serving.

Tips & Common Questions

Why start skin-side down?

Starting skin-side down lets the fat render directly against the hot basket, which helps crisp it before flipping — starting skin-up first can leave the skin softer.

Do I need to preheat the air fryer?

Preheating gives a more consistent sear from the start — skipping it usually just adds a couple minutes to the total cook time rather than ruining the result.

Can I cook them in a single crowded layer to save time?

No — overlapping pieces block air circulation, which is what makes an air fryer crisp food in the first place. Cook in batches if your basket is small.
