Chicken Tacos

By Laura Bennett · Published 2026-07-18 · 28g protein per serving

Taco night doesn't need much beyond well-seasoned chicken and a hot pan — the spice blend here does the heavy lifting, so there's no marinating required.

Diced chicken cooks fast and browns well, giving good texture without the wait of a whole seasoned chicken breast.

Chicken Tacos

Prep: 10 min Cook: 15 min Total: 30 min Yield: 4 servings (8 tacos) 320 cal · 28g protein

Ingredients

  • 1.5 lbs boneless chicken breast, diced
  • 2 tsp chili powder
  • 1 tsp cumin
  • 1 tsp smoked paprika
  • 1/2 tsp garlic powder
  • 1/2 tsp onion powder
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 8 small tortillas, warmed
  • Shredded lettuce, diced tomato, shredded cheese, sour cream, for serving
  • Lime wedges, for serving

Instructions

  1. 1. Season the chicken

    Toss diced chicken with chili powder, cumin, smoked paprika, garlic powder, onion powder and a pinch of salt.

  2. 2. Cook

    Heat olive oil in a large skillet over medium-high heat. Add chicken in a single layer and cook 7-8 minutes, stirring occasionally, until browned and cooked through.

  3. 3. Assemble

    Divide chicken among warmed tortillas. Top with lettuce, tomato, cheese and sour cream.

  4. 4. Serve

    Serve with lime wedges for squeezing over the top.

Tips & Common Questions

How do I warm tortillas without them cracking?

Warm them directly in a dry skillet for 20-30 seconds per side, or wrap a stack in foil and heat in a 300°F oven for 10 minutes — cold tortillas straight from the fridge are what crack when folded.

Can I use ground chicken instead of diced breast?

Yes — ground chicken cooks faster (about 5-6 minutes) and works well with the same seasoning blend, just break it apart as it browns.

Can I make the chicken ahead for meal prep?

Yes — the seasoned cooked chicken keeps in the fridge for up to 4 days, making it easy to assemble tacos fresh each night without recooking.
